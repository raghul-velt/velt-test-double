Meridian Outfitters: Brand and Product Copy Standards

Version 2.1 · Applies to the Meridian Outfitters online store · Owner: Brand and Merchandising · Scope: the Meridian store only

1. Brand name and voice

  1. Always write the brand as "Meridian Outfitters" in full on first mention on every page, and "Meridian" alone thereafter. Never write "meridian outfitters" in lowercase or the singular "Meridian Outfitter".
  2. Always write in a calm, plain voice. Never use "cheap", "bargain", "deal", or "steal" to describe a product or a price. If a price must be described, write "a fair price".
  3. Never use exclamation marks anywhere on the store, including product names, banners, and buttons.
  4. Never use urgency phrases such as "Hurry", "Only a few left", "Limited time", or "Act now".
  5. Always use United States English spelling. Never use "colour", "favourite", "grey", or other British variants.

2. Products and prices

  1. Always show every price with the currency code and two decimals, for example "USD 148.00". Never show a bare dollar sign or a price without decimals.
  2. Never end a price in .99 or .95. Meridian prices are whole dollar amounts.
  3. Always show the sentence "Free returns within 60 days" on every product card and every product page.
  4. Always write product names in Title Case, for example "Ridgeline Down Parka".
  5. Always list apparel sizes in the order XS, S, M, L, XL. Never list sizes in any other order and never use numeric sizes for apparel.
  6. Always label the purchase button "Add to bag". Never use "Add to cart", "Buy now", or "Shop now" on a purchase button.
  7. Always state the fabric or material composition on every product page, for example "100 percent recycled nylon".

3. Color and typography

  1. Always use forest green #1F4D3A for primary buttons, text links, and the wordmark. Never use purple, blue, or red as an accent.
  2. Always use bone #F3EFE6 as the page background. Never use pure white #FFFFFF as a page background.
  3. Always set headings in Fraunces, a serif, and body copy in Work Sans. Never use a geometric sans-serif for headings.
  4. Always use a 2 pixel corner radius on buttons. Never use pill-shaped or fully rounded buttons.

4. Imagery and layout

  1. Always show products photographed on a plain bone background. Never use lifestyle photography on a product card.
  2. Always give every product image alternative text that names the product and its color.
  3. Always show the product grid in three columns on desktop. Never show more than three columns.
  4. Always place the sustainability statement "Made to be repaired, not replaced" in the footer of every page.

5. Journal and contact

  1. Always show the author and the publish date on every journal entry. Never publish an entry without an author.
  2. Always format the store address as "88 Harbor Street, Portland, ME 04101".
  3. Always format the customer service hours as "Mon to Sat, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM ET". Never use a hyphen between days or times.
  4. Always make the customer service telephone number "(207) 555-0188" a working tel: link.
  5. Always label the contact form submit button "Send message".

Questions about these standards go to brand@meridianoutfitters.example. This document supersedes version 2.0 and applies to the Meridian store only; it does not apply to other properties in the workspace.
