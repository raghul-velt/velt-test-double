Meridian Outfitters repairs outdoor gear at the Portland workshop. Bring jackets, tents, packs and boots; most repairs are ready within two weeks, and we call you before ordering any part we do not stock.

Drop-off hours are Tuesday to Saturday, ten in the morning to six in the evening. Parking is free behind the building.
