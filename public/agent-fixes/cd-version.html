Repairs and returns

Meridian Outfitters repairs jackets, tents, backpacks and boots in our own workshop. Most repairs are ready within two weeks, and we call you before ordering any part that is not in stock. Bring the item to any store with your receipt, or send it to us with the prepaid label from your account page.

Returns are free within thirty days for unused items in their original packaging. Used items can be returned within the same window if they are faulty, and we will repair, replace or refund them at your choice. Gift cards and personalised items cannot be returned.

Our workshop is open Monday to Saturday, from ten in the morning until six in the evening. On public holidays the workshop is closed, and repairs that were due that day are ready on the next working day instead.
