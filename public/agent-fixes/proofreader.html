Spring Repair Clinic

Our spring repair clinic opens on Febuary 12 at the Portland workshop. Bring a jacket with a torn seam, a tent with a broken pole sleeve, or a pack with a tired zip, and our team will fix it while you wait.

This year we are also running the clinic at our new shop in Edinburh, our first store outside Maine. The Scottish team will take bookings from the first week of March.

Every TrailMax shell and Northline parka we have made since 2019 can be repaired for free. Older Meridian pieces are repaired at cost, and we will tell you the price before we start.

You will recieve a text when your gear is ready to collect.

Meridian Outfitters, Portland, Maine.
