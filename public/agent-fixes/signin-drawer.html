The Winter Shell Collection

Three shells for the coldest weeks of the year, each cut long in the back and finished with taped seams. The Harbor shell is the lightest of the three and packs into its own chest pocket.

The Northline parka adds a quilted liner and a hood that fits over a climbing helmet. The Ridge shell sits between them: a fleece-backed collar, two hand pockets and an inside pocket sized for a phone.

Every piece in the collection can be sent back to the Portland workshop for repair, for as long as you own it. We patch, re-tape and replace zips, and we tell you the price before we start.

Sizes run from extra small to triple extra large, and every size is stocked in all three colors through the end of February.
